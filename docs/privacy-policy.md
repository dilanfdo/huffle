Huffle ("we", "our", or "us") is a gentle movement-habit app for Android, published by
NexusDigitalLabs. You log the movement you did, and a creature called a Huffling grows with you. This
Privacy Policy explains what information Huffle handles, how it is used, and the choices you have.

## Summary
Huffle works entirely on your device. There is no account, no sign-up, no advertising, and no analytics
or crash-reporting SDK. Your data is never sent to NexusDigitalLabs. If you choose to turn on the
optional Google Drive backup, a copy of your data is saved to a hidden folder in your own Google Drive,
which only Huffle and you can use.

## Information Stored on Your Device
To work, Huffle stores the following on your device only: the name you give your Huffling, the sessions
you log (the type of movement, the time, and an optional duration and note), your streak and progress
(XP, evolution stage, streak-freeze tokens), your weekly goal, your reminder time, and which cosmetic
item your Huffling is wearing. This information stays on your device. We do not run a server that
receives it and we cannot see it.

## Notifications
Huffle can send you a daily reminder and occasional streak and comeback nudges. These are scheduled
locally on your device. Huffle does not use remote push notifications and does not collect a push or
device token. On Android 13 and later you are asked for permission, and you can turn notifications off at
any time in your device settings.

## Optional Google Drive Backup
If you tap "Connect Google Drive" in Settings, Huffle asks you to sign in with your Google account and to
allow access to the app's own hidden application-data folder in your Drive. Huffle uses this to save a
backup file of your Huffling data and to restore it on a new phone. Huffle can only see that one folder:
it cannot see, read, or change any of your other Drive files. The backup goes directly between your
device and Google; it is not sent to NexusDigitalLabs. Huffle shows the email address of the connected
Google account inside the app so you can see which account is in use, and does not store or transmit it
elsewhere.

You can disconnect at any time in Settings, and you can delete the backup from your Google account
(Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Huffle). Huffle's use and transfer of information received from
Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## Device Backups
Android may include Huffle's data in your device backup to your Google account, depending on your device
settings. That backup is handled by Android and Google under their own terms, not by Huffle.

## Advertising, Analytics and Purchases
Huffle contains no advertising, no third-party analytics, and no tracking. It currently offers no in-app
purchases. If optional purchases are introduced in the future, this policy will be updated before they
are available.

## Google Play Diagnostics
If you have opted in on your device, Google Play may collect crash and performance diagnostics (Android
vitals) about apps you use, including Huffle. This is collected by Google under its own policies and is
available to us only in aggregate through the Play Console.

## Data Retention & Deletion
Because your data lives on your device, you control it. Uninstalling Huffle, or clearing its storage in
your device settings, removes it from the device. If you used Google Drive backup, you can also remove
the backup from your Google account as described above. We hold no copy of your data, so there is
nothing for us to delete on our side.

## Your Rights
Depending on where you live (including under GDPR and CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct,
delete, or restrict personal data. Since we do not hold your Huffle data, these are exercised directly on
your device and in your Google account. You can still contact us with any question or request.

## Children's Privacy
Huffle is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from
children.

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an
updated date.

## Contact
