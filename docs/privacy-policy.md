# Huffle Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [date]_

Huffle is a habit app published by Nexus Digital Labs. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## What Huffle stores
Everything you enter — your Huffling's name, the sessions you log (type, optional duration and note), your streak, and your settings — is stored **only on your device**.

## What Huffle does not do
- It does not create an account or ask for your name, email, or phone number.
- It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your data with us or anyone else.
- It does not include advertising or third-party analytics.
- It does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.

## Notifications
Huffle can send you a daily reminder and an occasional "come back" nudge. These are scheduled locally on your device. You can turn them off in the app's Settings or in your device's notification settings.

## Backups
If you have device backup turned on, your phone's operating system may include Huffle's data in your backup (for example, Android Auto Backup with your Google account). That is controlled by your device settings and the operating system provider, not by Huffle.

## Children
Huffle is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same address with a new date.
